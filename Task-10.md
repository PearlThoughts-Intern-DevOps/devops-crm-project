## 5. CloudWatch Agent Installation and Configuration

The CloudWatch Agent was installed and configured on the EC2 instance.

The agent was configured to collect operating-system-level resource metrics.

The following metrics were configured:

* CPU utilization
* Memory utilization
* Disk utilization

The custom metrics were published under the `CWAgent` namespace.

## 7. CloudWatch Alarms

CloudWatch alarms were configured to monitor important resource thresholds.

### CPU Alarm

**Alarm Name:** `High-CPU-Alarm-PT`

* Metric: `cpu_usage_active`
* Statistic: Average
* Period: 5 minutes
* Threshold: Greater than 70%
* Evaluation: 1 datapoint

### Memory Alarm

**Alarm Name:** `High-Memory-Alarm-PT`

* Metric: `mem_used_percent`
* Statistic: Average
* Period: 5 minutes
* Threshold: Greater than 70%
* Evaluation: 1 datapoint

### Disk Alarm

**Alarm Name:** `High-Disk-Alarm-PT`

* Metric: `disk_used_percent`
* Statistic: Average
* Period: 5 minutes
* Threshold: Greater than 80%
* Evaluation: 1 datapoint

All three alarms were verified successfully and reached the **OK** state.

## 11. Issue and Solution

### CloudWatch Alarm Initially Showed Insufficient Data

Immediately after creating the CloudWatch alarms, the CPU alarm initially displayed **Insufficient data** because CloudWatch required sufficient datapoints from the CloudWatch Agent.

**Solution:**
Waited for the CloudWatch Agent to publish new datapoints and refreshed the CloudWatch console. The alarm subsequently changed to **OK** and was verified successfully.
